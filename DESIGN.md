# Lakay Toussaint Community Alliance Design System

This `DESIGN.md` is an agent-facing brand and UI design reference based only on the provided logo, typography, color palette, and logo variation images. Use it to keep future designs visually consistent with the Lakay Toussaint Community Alliance identity.

## Brand Essence

Lakay Toussaint Community Alliance should feel bold, rooted, welcoming, and community-centered. The visual identity combines the idea of home with Haitian heritage through a house symbol, the outline of Haiti, and a strong red, navy, gold, green, and light gray palette.

Design work should communicate:

- Home, belonging, and community care.
- Haitian identity and cultural pride.
- Trust, stability, and civic presence.
- Warmth, action, and collective support.

Avoid visual directions that feel generic, overly corporate, overly delicate, or disconnected from the logo's strong community-home symbolism.

## Logo System

The logo mark contains a house in front of the outline of Haiti. The house uses a red body, navy roof, white windows, green land/outline shape, and gold edging. The wordmark reads `LAKAY TOUSSAINT` in bold uppercase lettering, with `COMMUNITY ALLIANCE` set below in a lighter, widely spaced uppercase style.

### Approved Logo Variations

Use these variations as shown in the provided reference images:

- Full horizontal lockup: icon on the left, wordmark on the right.
- Stacked lockup: icon above the wordmark.
- Compact stacked lockup: icon and wordmark arranged for square or narrow spaces.
- White one-color logo on solid navy, red, green, or gold backgrounds.
- Icon-only mark when space is too limited for the full name.

### Logo Usage Rules

- Preserve the relationship between the house, Haiti outline, and wordmark.
- Do not redraw, distort, stretch, crop, or rotate the logo.
- Do not rearrange the icon and type outside the approved lockups.
- Do not add shadows, bevels, glows, extra outlines, or new effects.
- Do not place the full-color logo on busy photography or low-contrast backgrounds.
- Use the white logo version on solid brand-color backgrounds.
- Keep clear space around the logo equal to at least the height of the house windows.
- When the logo appears small, use the stacked or icon-only version for legibility.

## Color Palette

Use the corrected brand colors below. These values supersede any ambiguous or mismatched printed labels in earlier reference images.

| Color | Hex | Primary Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Red | `#CD1223` | Brand accent, calls to action, house body, urgency |
| Navy | `#003459` | Primary text/wordmark color, structure, dark backgrounds |
| Gold | `#EFC577` | Logo edging, highlights, celebratory accents |
| Green | `#17590F` | Haiti outline, growth, community, heritage accents |
| Light Gray | `#EAEAEA` | Neutral backgrounds and soft surfaces |

Use these HEX values exactly in new design work unless the brand owner provides a newer palette.

### Color Use

- Use light gray as the main neutral background color.
- Use navy for strong headings, structural elements, and serious brand moments.
- Use red for important calls to action and emphasis.
- Use green to reference the Haiti shape, growth, care, and community.
- Use gold as an accent, outline, divider, or highlight rather than a default text color.
- Use white for the one-color logo on saturated red, navy, green, or gold backgrounds.

### Color Pairing

Preferred pairings:

- Navy text on light gray.
- Red accents on light gray.
- White logo on navy.
- White logo on red.
- White logo on green.
- White logo on gold.
- Gold accents around navy or red elements.

Avoid:

- Low-contrast text combinations.
- Gold body text on light gray.
- Red and green competing at equal strength in large areas.
- Adding unapproved blues, purples, oranges, creams, or grays as dominant brand colors.

## Typography

The brand board identifies two typefaces:

- `League Spartan` for `LAKAY TOUSSAINT`.
- `Garet` for `COMMUNITY ALLIANCE`.

### Type Roles

Use `League Spartan` for:

- Main headings.
- Hero statements.
- Page titles.
- Strong calls to action.
- Short brand-forward labels.

Use `Garet` for:

- Body copy.
- Subheadings.
- Navigation.
- Captions.
- Small uppercase labels.
- Supporting text such as `COMMUNITY ALLIANCE`.

### Type Styling

- Set major headings in bold uppercase or strong title case.
- Keep `LAKAY TOUSSAINT` bold, compact, and confident.
- Set `COMMUNITY ALLIANCE` in uppercase with wide letter spacing.
- Use generous line height for body copy.
- Avoid script fonts, serif display fonts, thin luxury fonts, or playful rounded fonts.
- Do not use negative letter spacing.

Recommended typography direction:

- Headlines: bold, geometric, high-impact.
- Supporting text: clean, open, and readable.
- Small labels: uppercase with wide tracking.

## Visual Style

The visual system should be strong, clean, and brand-led.

Use:

- Solid brand-color blocks.
- Light gray or white space around the full-color logo.
- Bold section titles.
- Simple geometric layouts.
- Clear visual hierarchy.
- Modest borders or dividers in gold or light gray.
- White logo marks on saturated brand backgrounds.

Avoid:

- Decorative gradients that are not present in the brand board.
- Excessive shadows or glass effects.
- Overly rounded, soft, or playful UI styling.
- Thin-line icons that clash with the bold logo.
- Cluttered backgrounds behind the logo.

## Layout Guidance

Design layouts should give the logo and palette room to breathe.

- Use wide margins around the logo.
- Keep compositions centered or strongly aligned.
- Use clear sections with generous spacing.
- Favor simple grids over complex collage layouts.
- Let red, navy, green, and gold appear as intentional brand fields or accents.
- Use light gray as a calm canvas for content-heavy pages.

For formal or official materials, use the horizontal logo lockup when there is enough width. For social, square, mobile, or small surfaces, use the stacked lockup.

## UI Components

### Buttons

Primary action buttons should use a saturated brand color, preferably red or navy, with white text.

Secondary buttons can use light gray or white backgrounds with navy text and a navy or gold border.

Button labels should be short, direct, and action-oriented.

### Cards And Panels

Cards should be simple and structured:

- Light gray or white background.
- Navy headings.
- Red or gold accent line.
- Minimal shadow or no shadow.
- Clean spacing.

### Navigation

Navigation should feel clear and grounded:

- Use Garet or a similar clean sans-serif.
- Keep links readable and direct.
- Use navy as the main navigation color.
- Highlight the most important action in red or navy.

## Imagery Direction

When using photography or illustration with this brand, imagery should support the logo's themes of home, Haiti, and community.

Preferred imagery:

- Homes and community spaces.
- Haitian community gatherings.
- Volunteers, families, and neighbors.
- Cultural pride and shared celebration.
- Rebuilding, care, and mutual support.

Image treatment:

- Keep images warm and natural.
- Avoid heavy filters.
- Avoid placing detailed imagery directly behind the logo.
- If the logo must appear over an image, place it on a clean solid-color block or use a high-contrast overlay.

## Accessibility

- Maintain strong contrast between text and background.
- Use white text on red, navy, green, or gold only when contrast is sufficient.
- Use navy or dark text on light gray.
- Do not rely on color alone to communicate meaning.
- Keep small uppercase text large enough and spaced enough to remain readable.
- Preserve the logo's legibility at all sizes.

## Agent Checklist

Before creating any design from this brand:

1. Use the provided logo system without modification.
2. Use only the brand palette from this document unless instructed otherwise.
3. Use League Spartan for strong brand headings.
4. Use Garet for supporting type and body content.
5. Keep layouts bold, clean, and community-centered.
6. Use light gray, white, or solid brand-color backgrounds behind logo applications.
7. Avoid effects, colors, or fonts that are not represented in the reference images.

When in doubt, prioritize the clearest expression of the provided brand board: bold logo, clean light-gray space, strong navy type, red action, green heritage, and gold accenting.
