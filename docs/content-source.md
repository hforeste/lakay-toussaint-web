## Organization Copy

### Tagline

Building community here. Rebuilding home there.

### Mission Statement

No Haitian Left Behind.

### Mission

Lakay Toussaint Community Alliance builds collective power through education, economic empowerment, direct service, justice, and Haiti rebuilding.

### Vision

A thriving Haitian community where no one is left behind, empowered with knowledge, economic opportunity, and justice here in Seattle, while actively rebuilding and strengthening Haiti for generations to come.

## Name Heritage

`Lakay` means home in Kreyol. It represents creating community and home here in Seattle while actively rebuilding home in Haiti.

`Toussaint` honors Toussaint Louverture, Haiti's founding father and liberator, whose legacy reflects vision, education, freedom, and service to the people.

`Community Alliance` means building collective power through partnership. Together, Lakay Toussaint is home, heritage, and a call to serve.

## Values

- **Service over self:** We put community needs ahead of individual gain and build programs that meet people where they are.
- **Community over gain:** We believe Haitian families rise together through trust, partnership, and shared responsibility.
- **Heritage and dignity:** We preserve Haitian history, culture, language, and traditions with pride and care.
- **Justice:** We advocate for equity, civil rights, and systemic change that affects Haitian families.
- **Economic opportunity:** We support entrepreneurship, financial literacy, professional growth, and wealth-building.
- **Home here and there:** We build belonging in Seattle while supporting development and resilience in Haiti.

## Five Pillars

The website and data exports should use these five pillars consistently:

1. **Education** (`education`)
2. **Economic Empowerment** (`economic-empowerment`)
3. **Service** (`service`)
4. **Justice** (`justice`)
5. **Haiti Rebuilding** (`haiti-rebuilding`)

## Pillar Details

- **Education:** Academic support, professional development, college prep, and scholarship access.
- **Economic Empowerment:** Financial literacy, entrepreneurship, job placement, and wealth-building.
- **Service:** Food assistance, housing support, emergency relief, and community resources.
- **Justice:** Civil rights advocacy, policy work, community organizing, and systemic change.
- **Haiti Rebuilding:** Infrastructure, development projects, strategic partnerships, and direct investment.
